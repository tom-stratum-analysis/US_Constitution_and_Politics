# Speaker Notes — Lecture 20: Court Cases and Bill of Rights Part 2

Criminal-justice half. Speech and civil-rights cases come next.

## Empowerment

Most criminal law is state law, so incorporation is how the Bill of Rights reaches the government that actually arrests people.

## Barron and incorporation

*Barron v. Baltimore* (1833): BoR is a limit on the federal government. *Gitlow* (1925) begins speech incorporation. The rest is selective. Grand jury indictment (5th) and civil jury (7th) are the usual "not incorporated" examples.

## Mapp / Gideon / Miranda

- *Mapp*: exclusionary rule applied to the states.
- *Gideon*: appointed counsel as a Sixth Amendment right against the states.
- *Miranda*: warnings are a Fifth Amendment rule for custodial interrogation.

Pattern: these cases attach the rights to *state* prosecutors. That is where the force is used.
