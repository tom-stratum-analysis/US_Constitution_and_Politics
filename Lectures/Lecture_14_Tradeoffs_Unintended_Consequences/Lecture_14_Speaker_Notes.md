# Speaker Notes — Lecture 14: Trade-offs and Unintended Consequences

## Empowerment

Pattern recognition and a habit of asking what is the other side of this ledger.

## Federalist / knowledge problem

Even if every official were perfectly virtuous, the knowledge problem remains. Trade-offs exist even with perfect knowledge. The knowledge problem makes the trade-offs harder to see in advance.

## Teaching sequence

Personal trade-off opener first. Hancock and Garner pair high taxes on a desired good with enforcement by force. Cobra effect is the clean visual. Unintended good (emancipation after a secession vote intended to protect slavery) does not wash intended harm.

## Closing

We limit government not only because of the danger of force, but because of the limits of knowledge and the reality of trade-offs.
