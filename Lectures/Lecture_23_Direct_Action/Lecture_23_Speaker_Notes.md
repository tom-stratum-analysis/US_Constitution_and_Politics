# Speaker Notes — Lecture 23: Direct Action

Voting is the most familiar civic act. It is not the most powerful one.

## Toolkit

Speech = message. Press = amplifier (includes a phone). Assembly = group power. Petition = the ask.

## Spectrum and risk

Do not romanticize civil disobedience and do not sneer at email campaigns. Contact is low risk / high effort. Filming is legally protected and situationally dangerous. Civil disobedience: arrest is the point; First Amendment coverage ends when the law is broken on purpose.

## Turner v. Driver

Fort Worth, filming a police station from a public sidewalk, 5th Circuit 2017. "Basic, vital, and well-established liberty." Texas/5th Circuit illustration of a general First Amendment point.
