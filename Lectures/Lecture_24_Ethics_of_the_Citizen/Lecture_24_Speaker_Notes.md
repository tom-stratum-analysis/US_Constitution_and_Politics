# Speaker Notes — Lecture 24: Ethics of the Citizen

Capstone is voting-heavy. Add one Direct Action responsibility question — one prompt, not a second lecture.

## Delegation

If the John Wick still is in assets, ask who is culpable: client, hired killer, or both. Almost no one lets the client off. Then: we are the clients.

## Brennan

Keep the quote. Layers: voting for leaders; leaders writing coercive policy; officials using force; taxes paying for the force.

## Reciprocity

Hillel stays as a historically important quote. Teaching line: what is hateful to you, do not vote for the government to do to your neighbor.

## Secession case

Already used in Trade-offs as unintended emancipation. Today the question is the voters' intention and knowledge. Unintended good does not wash the intended defense of slavery. Criticizing a vote is not an argument for taking the franchise away.
