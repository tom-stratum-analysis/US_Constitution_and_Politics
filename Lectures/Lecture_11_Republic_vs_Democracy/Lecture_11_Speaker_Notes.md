# Lecture 11 Speaker Notes — Republic vs Democracy

**Next:** Congress constraining the executive

## Empowerment

Explain why republics/democracies arose against abusive executives; define both; show representative government + minority rights (including property) as one project.

## Why

Problem is the executive: king → tyrant. Stationary bandit has incentive to protect production to tax it; still predation unless constrained. Locke: legitimate government secures life, liberty, property — must not be the chief bandit.

## Definitions

Democracy = rule by the people. Republic = res publica = the public thing. "Republic = not monarchy" is largely a labeling accident; both were anti-tyranny projects.

## Aristotle and Madison

Democracy as corrupt form = many ruling for themselves. Representation and a larger sphere filter faction.
