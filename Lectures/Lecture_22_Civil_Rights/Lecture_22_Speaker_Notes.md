Do not force a winner. Two strategies for the same problem: a government of force that did not treat a class of people as dignified equals.

## Liberties vs rights

Liberties = limits on organized coercive force. Rights in this lecture = equal application, plus equal access to the ordinary incidents of membership. Early movement = "you already promised this; keep it."

Douglass image is a callback to Lecture 7.

## Discussion

"By any means necessary" vs dignified equals is the hard one. A society of equals cannot be built by tools that permanently sort people into rulers and ruled — and it also cannot ignore that the existing order was already using force.
