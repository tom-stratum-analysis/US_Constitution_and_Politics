## Empowerment

Distinguish misinformation from disinformation. Spot how left-coded budget memes misuse the discretionary base. Spot right-coded fabricated quotes and confused trade claims. Explain why bad information undermines both persuasion and wise limits on government.

## Left-coded: defense budget

Viral "55% defense" is on the discretionary slice (~27% of the federal budget), not the total. 55% × ~27% ≈ 15% of total federal. With state/local, defense share of all government spending is closer to 8–10%.

If the underlying policy goal is sound, massively misleading numbers still destroy credibility.

## Right-coded: fabricated quote and trade

The AOC-and-guns meme is fabricated. Same disease, other team.

U.S. importers pay duties to Customs; costs largely pass to U.S. buyers. A money trade deficit is a goods surplus.
