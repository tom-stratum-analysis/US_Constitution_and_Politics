# Speaker Notes — USGOVT101 Lecture 03: Basic Ethics

## Goals

1. Students can state a personal standard for when force is acceptable and apply it to government.
2. Link Lecture 2 definition (organized coercive physical force + legitimacy) to ethics.
3. Trolley → reciprocity → formal labels → trusteeship → delegated violence.
4. Leave time for personal-ethics discussion.
5. Aristotle moved to Lecture 4.

## Opening

If government *is* coercive force, ethics is the subject, not a side topic.

## Trolley

Image first; narrate the classic setup. Word slides (Utility / Omission vs Commission / Ends and Means) — expand verbally. Discussion: lever or not; does the ratio change the answer?

## Golden Rule block

Read each quote; let attribution sit. Do not rush. Quote-only is fine for tradition-sensitive figures.

## Fiduciary duties

Seven name-only slides — fill meaning. Punch line: money-power vs force-power standards.

## Wick

Client or hired force? Ties to citizens / officials and delegated state violence.

## Discussion

Personal standards; balance of rules vs consequences. When is violence justified? Collect; do not grade "correct" ethics.
