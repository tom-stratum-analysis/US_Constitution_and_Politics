## Empowerment

Place the Founding on a spectrum of how much we should limit government, and state two modern classical-liberal restatements (Coyne; individual fulfillment).

## Bridge

Lecture 2 (government = organized coercive force accepted as legitimate) plus Lecture 3 (ethics of force). Founding values answer: how do we live together peacefully as dignified equals? Slavery, exclusion, and Spooner-style consent belong later. Today is the aim of the thinking.

## Classical liberalism

Not modern party labels. Enlightenment package: limit power, expand who participates, let individuals and voluntary associations flourish.

Coyne restatement: live together peacefully in a society of dignified equals.

Fulfillment restatement: empowering individuals to pursue their own fulfillment — the person, not the collective.

## Spectrum of limits

Thoreau: government that governs not at all. "Governs least" is *The United States Magazine and Democratic Review*, not Thoreau. Krauthammer: keep organized violence at the lowest possible level. Constitutional limits: force only when and how the constitution allows.

## Hard cases
