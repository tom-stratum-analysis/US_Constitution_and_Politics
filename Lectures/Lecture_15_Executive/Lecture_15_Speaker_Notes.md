# Speaker Notes — Lecture 15: The Executive

Spine: Necessary Leviathan. Do not cover the whole Article II list.

## Empowerment

Students should leave able to state both sides in one breath: we need an executive; that is also why we fear one.

## Where this fits

Congress lectures: who writes rules and holds the purse. Trade-offs: knowledge problems in design. Today: implementation. Police, prosecutors, and the military are where policy becomes coercion.

## Necessary Leviathan

A constitution that cannot be enforced is a suggestion. “Leviathan” is not praise of unlimited power; some concentrated force is the price of living together.

## Defender / Take Care

Faithfully execute *the laws Congress made*, not invent a parallel legal system.

## Despoiler

Selective enforcement, emergency stretching, using the military or justice system against opponents, ignoring the purse.

## Enforcement as choice

No executive can enforce every law equally. Priorities are policy. Personal removal is rare; money is daily.
