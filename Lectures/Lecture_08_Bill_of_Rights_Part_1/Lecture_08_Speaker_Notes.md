# Lecture 8 Speaker Notes — Bill of Rights Part 1

**Course:** USGOVT101

## Empowerment

"Congress shall make no law" originally bound only the federal government until incorporation. Religion clauses barred a national establishment while protecting free exercise and leaving state establishments intact. The home is a repeated special zone. There is no general privacy right in the text.

## Federal-only scope

*Barron v. Baltimore* (1833). Changes begin later via 14th Amendment incorporation. Preview only.

## Religion

No national church, no birth membership, no national tax support, no federal clergy salaries. 1776: all 13 had establishments; VA 1786, NH 1817, MA 1833. "Separation of church and state" is not in the text.

## Home sequence

Third, Fourth, and the property side of the Fifth repeatedly treat the home as special even without a free-floating privacy clause.

## Privacy discussion

Should we pass a right-to-privacy amendment? What should it say? How and where should it apply?
