# Lecture 9 Speaker Notes — Better Angels

**Course:** USGOVT101

## Empowerment

Order-of-magnitude improvements in violence, life expectancy, and extreme poverty. Availability / "bad news is news" explains why the crisis narrative feels true. High baselines of peace and prosperity argue for caution before drastic coercive action.

Names (Pinker, OWID, Kurzweil, EPA, Eisner): speaker notes only unless a student asks.

## Verbal weave

Vivid recent events fill the news; gradual improvement does not. Students over-weight what is available in memory.

## Discussion bridge

1. Government as organized coercive force
2. Ethics of the use of force
3. Classical liberal challenge: live together peacefully as dignified equals
4. Overstatement of problems amid human progress
