# Speaker Notes — Lecture 16: Federal Reserve and Independent Agencies

Spine: Douglass North / credible commitment. Debt-ceiling brinkmanship is the illustration, not the whole lecture.

## Speak the tie-in

North: honoring debts lowers the cost of capital. Full faith and credit is a public good. Predictable public finance is load-bearing.

Glorious Revolution → parliamentary control of finance → credible public debt → cheaper borrowing. Fed as fiscal agent is the American cousin, not a secret owner of the government.

## Independence

Congress sets the dual mandate. Fed chooses means. Governors: 14-year staggered terms. Independent *within* government.

## Conspiracy block

Stock exists, is mandatory, capped dividend, no residual ownership. Excess earnings to Treasury. Board is a federal agency. “Audit the Fed” usually means remove the GAO carve-out on policy judgments — not “never audited.” Jekyll Island was secretive; the Act was then debated and passed. Policy mistakes are real. Family-ownership is not.
