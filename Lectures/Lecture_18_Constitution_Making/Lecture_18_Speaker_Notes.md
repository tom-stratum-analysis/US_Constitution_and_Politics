# Speaker Notes — Lecture 18: Constitution Making

Opens Part 4: countermajoritarian institutions and the minority’s stake.

**Reading to mention:** James M. Buchanan, “The Constitution of Economic Policy” (1986 Nobel lecture / *Science* 1987). Rules of the game vs. plays within the game. Hold Federalist 78 and Brutus XI/XV for Courts.

## Why losers stay

1. A real chance to win later.
2. Fundamental rights that survive an electoral loss.

If the winner can rewrite the rulebook every Tuesday, peaceful rotation dies.

## Electoral College

States as states choose the president. Classification question, not a campaign-season seminar.

## Filibuster

Senate rule, not a clause. Can protect a minority or veto a majority that already cleared the Constitution’s own hurdles. Separate “I like the outcomes it blocks” from “this is how the written Constitution works.”
