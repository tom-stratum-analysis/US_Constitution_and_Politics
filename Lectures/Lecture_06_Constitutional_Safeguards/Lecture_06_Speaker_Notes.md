**Course:** USGOVT101

## Empowerment

Name the structural tools that limit organized coercive force; the point is splitting power so no one dominates alone.

## Separation and checks

Use the three graphics. Build: separate → balance → check → no one acts alone. Article VI quote anchors judicial review without a full court history.

## Into federalism

Bicameral Congress is the bridge. Declaration’s “States” plural.

Independent-countries point: thirteen originals + Vermont + Texas + Hawaii were independent; other states were purchased or won in war but still receive the federalism bargain. Texas is a local illustration, not a full Texas institutions unit.

## Federalism

Tenth Amendment as the reservation rule. Most ordinary crime, property, contracts, family, and intrastate life stay with the states. If it is not granted, it is not federal.
