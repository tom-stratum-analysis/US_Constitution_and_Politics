# Speaker Notes — Lecture 17: War Powers and Foreign Policy

Callback: in 1789 war was still George III’s prerogative in Britain. Declaration in Article I; command in Article II. Keep later cases as *patterns*. Students will arrive with partisan scripts.

## WPR 1973

Consultation “in every possible instance,” 48-hour report, 60-day clock plus 30-day withdrawal. After *INS v. Chadha* (1983), a concurrent resolution is a political signal, not an enforceable command. A joint resolution that survives veto is different.

Libya 2011 is the older precedent for “no ground troops / low risk to U.S. personnel, therefore maybe not hostilities.”

## Pattern, not party

The institutional pattern is older than this administration. Capture operations and the 60-day clock are patterns, not verdicts.
